ZION PREMIUM

Subscription &
Activation Guide

Follow these simple steps to purchase ZION PREMIUM and activate your 30-day subscription.

01

Find a Premium Movie

Open the ZION home page. Movies that require a premium subscription will be marked with a PREMIUM badge on the poster.

PREMIUM
PREMIUM
02

Open the Premium Movie

Tap the movie marked PREMIUM. On the movie page, you will see the SUBSCRIBE TO WATCH option.

03

Buy from Account Center

You can also purchase ZION PREMIUM directly from your Account Center. Find the ZION PREMIUM banner and tap it to continue.

04

Enter Your Account Details

On the payment page, enter your email address and mobile number.

Important: The email address must be exactly the same email address registered on your ZION account.

05

Complete Your Payment

Select your preferred payment method and complete the payment securely.

Available payment methods may include UPI, Cards, Wallets, NetBanking and other methods supported by Razorpay.

06

Payment Confirmation

After successful payment, you will receive a payment confirmation page. Please read the confirmation message carefully.

PAYMENT CONFIRMATION

This is only a Razorpay payment confirmation message. Your payment has been received successfully.

Our team will carefully review and verify your payment within 24–48 hours. After successful verification, your ZION PREMIUM subscription will be activated for 30 days on your account.

IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ

The email address registered on your ZION account must exactly match the email address you provided while making the Razorpay payment.

If the email addresses do not match, your payment may be successfully completed, but your 30-day ZION PREMIUM subscription may not be activated.

Please make sure you always use the same email address for both your ZION account and Razorpay payment.

No refund will be provided for an email mismatch or incorrect account/payment details.

Thank you for choosing ZION PREMIUM.

07

Wait for Verification & Activation

After your payment is successfully verified by our team, ZION PREMIUM will be activated on your account for 30 days.

Please allow up to 24–48 hours for payment verification and activation.

Need Help?

If you experience any problem with your payment or subscription activation, contact ZION Support.
